1. Read aloud or sign the following passage:

A Visit to Mikumi National Park

In December, our school organised a trip to Mikumi National Park. We started the journey at early in the morning. On our way, we passed by Kibaha and Morogoro towns. We sang and cheered all the way. When we reached Mikumi, wildlife officers welcomed us. Then, a tour guide came to us and introduced herself. She said that she would be guiding us through the park. We were very excited to be there.
