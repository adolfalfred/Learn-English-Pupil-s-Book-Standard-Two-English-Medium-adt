I add sounds at the end of words.

Activity 1

1. Pronounce the words car , far and no . 2. Add a sound to the end of the words in (1).

Example: car-car d

3. Pronounce the new words you have formed in (2). 4. Read the following sentences aloud: (a) Every car has a card. (b) This tree is far from our farm. (c) No one will give a nod. (d) He parked a car near the cart. (e) There are ten people in the tent.

Enter the three words formed by adding a sound to the end of car, far and no. Use a keyboard or Braille to enter the response.
