Neema hurriedly walked into the sitting room. Mr Mouse was confused. He asked her, ’Where is my mouth?’ Neema was surprised. She pointed at her father’s mouth. Mr Mouse realised that he did not pronounce the word correctly. He repeated his question, ’Where is my mouse?’ Neema was even more surprised. She said to her father, ’Do you mean your name, father?’ Mr Mouse said, ’I want to play a game’. Neema replied, ’Oh! Father, I cannot catch a mouse’. Mr Mouse realised that the word has two meanings. He then said, ’I mean the computer mouse’. Neema understood. She told her father that she had not seen his mouse. Mr Mouse remembered he had forgotten it at the office.
