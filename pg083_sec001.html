Ms Hen and Mr Kite Ms Hen and Mr Kite were once good neighbours. One day, Ms Hen went to the Mr Kite’s home to borrow a sewing needle. Ms Hen wanted to sew clothes for her children. Mr Kite told Ms Hen not to lose the needle. Ms Hen agreed and took the needle home. After sewing the clothes, the needle got lost. Ms Hen was confused. She did not report the matter to Mr Kite. In the evening, Mr Kite came to Ms Hen’s house. He wanted his sewing needle back. Ms Hen started searching
